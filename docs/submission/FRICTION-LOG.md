# Friction Log

This log records real Amazon, AWS, Alexa, or competition-tool friction that may
later support the Amazon Build, Ship, Shape friction bonus.

It is not a general development diary. Grok, BDB, Windows, npm, or local
toolchain issues are out of scope unless they are directly an Amazon, AWS, or
Alexa competition-tool problem.

## Purpose

Capture exact, reproducible friction against Amazon/AWS/Alexa services and
tools so later submission evidence can distinguish real platform obstacles from
ordinary software-engineering work.

## Append-only policy

Entries are append-only by date. Existing entries are not rewritten to change
history. Corrections are added as later dated notes.

## Required future entry fields

Every qualifying entry must include:

- Date
- Tool / service
- Attempted task
- Exact steps
- Expected behavior
- Actual behavior
- Severity
- Workaround
- Actionable suggestion
